03

Complete Convocation Registration

Settle Fees

OFFICIAL GUIDE

Graduand Dress Code

Graduands are required to dress formally and appropriately throughout the Convocation Ceremony. Please refer to the official guide for the recommended attire and prohibited clothing.

  • Wear neat and appropriate formal attire.
  • Wear suitable formal shoes.
  • Avoid jeans, T-shirts, short skirts, sports shoes and slippers.
  • Ensure the complete academic robe is worn correctly.
PARENTS & GUESTS

Guest Dress Code

Parents and guests are encouraged to dress formally and respectfully in keeping with the significance of the Convocation Ceremony.

  • Formal or traditional attire is recommended.
  • Choose neat and appropriate footwear.
  • Avoid jeans, T-shirts, short skirts, sports shoes and slippers.
  • Please maintain appropriate attire throughout the ceremony.
!

Important Notes

  • Please ensure your attire is neat, formal and appropriate for a convocation ceremony.
  • Avoid casual wear, sportswear, denim, short skirts or revealing attire.
  • Graduands must wear the complete academic robe according to the University's requirements.
  • For special circumstances, please contact the Convocation Secretariat.
CEREMONY RULES

Do's & Don'ts

Keep the ceremony respectful and comfortable for everyone.

Watch the Official Convocation Ceremony Guide

Please watch the official Convocation Ceremony Guide before attending the ceremony.

▶ Watch Convocation Ceremony Guide on YouTube ↗
✓

Do

Things to do
  • Arrive according to the scheduled time.
  • Bring your complete academic robe set and required cards/passes.
  • Gather at the assigned location.
  • Follow the instructions of ceremony officers.
  • Remain in the hall until the ceremony is completed unless instructed otherwise.
×

Don't

Things not allowed
  • Do not bring food or drinks into the hall.
  • Do not bring unnecessary personal belongings or handbags into the hall.
  • Do not alter or add accessories to the academic robe.
  • Do not make inappropriate gestures on stage.
  • Do not leave before the ceremony ends without permission.
FOR FAMILY & GUESTS

Guest Information

Important information for guests attending the Convocation Ceremony.

01

Guest Admission

Each graduand is allowed to bring up to TWO (2) guests to the Convocation Ceremony.

02

Guest Pass

Guests must present their guest pass before entering the convocation hall.

03

Arrival Time

Guests are encouraged to arrive early for registration and seating.

04

Children

Children under 12 years old are not permitted to enter the ceremony hall. Graduands and guests are kindly requested to make the necessary arrangements before attending the ceremony.

05

During the Ceremony

Guests should remain seated and follow all instructions given by ceremony officers.

06

Guest Dress Code

Guests are expected to dress appropriately for the formal convocation ceremony. Please refer to the Dress Code section for guidance on suitable attire.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

Quick answers to common convocation questions.

Do I need to confirm my attendance?

Yes. Eligible graduands should complete the attendance confirmation and provide the required information.

How many guests can I bring?

Up to two guests, subject to the University's arrangements.

Can children enter the hall?

Children below 12 years old are not allowed to enter the hall.

Can I modify my academic robe?

No. Alterations or additional accessories on the academic robe are not allowed.

What should I bring to the ceremony?

Bring your complete academic robe set and required cards or guest passes, and follow the University's latest instructions.

What happens after the ceremony?

Return the complete academic robe and follow the University's instructions for collecting your official academic documents.

What if I have special needs?

Please indicate your requirements during attendance confirmation so the University can make the necessary arrangements.

Where can I find payment information?

See Fees & Payment. Bank and payment details will be updated once confirmed.
